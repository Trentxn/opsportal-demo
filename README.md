# OpsPortal demo

A preview of OpsPortal, an operational intelligence platform for power and water utilities.

**Open the demo: https://trentxn.github.io/opsportal-demo/**

It works on a phone as well as on a desktop. Choose an account on the first screen to see what that role can see and do.

## What you are looking at

This is the real OpsPortal web app, running entirely in your browser on a simulated fleet. Nothing is installed, nothing is sent to a server, and nothing you do is saved. Reload the page to start again.

The two utilities shown, Halcyon Islands Power and Tradewinds Water, their plants, their readings and their people are invented for the demo.

## What to try

* Open the **Overview** and select a unit in the fleet status panel.
* On an asset page, select a reading to chart it, and switch on **Compare with peers**.
* Open **Insights** to see what the AI service found and the evidence behind each finding.
* Acknowledge an alarm as the asset manager, then sign in as the viewer and notice what changes.
* Sign in as the super admin and switch between the two organizations.
* Switch between light and dark themes from the account menu.

## About this repository

It holds only the built files of the demo, published with GitHub Pages. The source code of OpsPortal is kept in a private repository.
